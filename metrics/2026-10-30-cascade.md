## What this means for the cascade

- On clear photos every tier reads every number correctly, so the cascade almost always stops at the first, cheapest tier.
- Wonder costs about 6× a minimal Spark read. It only runs when both Spark reads leave the numbers inconsistent (line sum ≠ total, missing price, quantity × price ≠ line total).
- Name differences are cosmetic (a trailing colon on a voucher line). They matter for alias matching, which is why the Review screen still lets the person confirm names.
- This sample cannot show where Wonder pays off. Faded, crumpled or long receipts from the field test are the real test; `receipts.extraction.tiers` records every tier tried per receipt.
